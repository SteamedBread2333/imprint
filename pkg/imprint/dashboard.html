This map is for relationships: who supersedes whom, what is related, what conflicts. It is not a spreadsheet of every claim. Pick a scope, then click a node. Press ? for the legend.

How to read this vault

Each node is one rule. Size is confidence. Colour is lifecycle. Lines are relationships written in frontmatter — not inferred similarity.

Filters (AND)
Every control must match. Scope means the rule’s tag list contains that tag. Search matches id, claim, or scope text. Clear chips or Esc to widen.
Local graph
Choosing a scope shows that cluster plus one hop of linked rules. graph all is the hairball — use it after you know what a colour means.
