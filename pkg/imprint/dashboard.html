This map is for relationships: who supersedes whom, what is related, what conflicts. It is not a spreadsheet of every claim. Pick a scope, then click a node. Press ? for the legend.

How to read this vault

The map is a dandelion drawn with D3: hub in the centre, rings of hops around it. Dot colour is scope. Lines are relationships written in frontmatter — not inferred similarity.

Filters (AND)
Every control must match. Scope means the rule’s tag list contains that tag. Search matches id, claim, or scope text. Clear chips or Esc to widen.
Dandelion
The hub is the busiest rule, or the one you click (it re-roots). Ring 1 = one hop. Same-scope seeds sit together on a ring so each colour is a petal. graph all draws every component as its own flower.
Nodes
Fill colour is scope. The corner legend is a palette of dots — hover for the name, click to filter. Full names stay in the left list. Border is lifecycle. Bigger = higher confidence.
